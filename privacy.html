Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy covers the Clarity Stack website (clairmont425.github.io/clarity-stack) and the internal publishing tools that Clarity Productions uses to manage its own social media profiles, including the Drip Drop shop's profiles. It is operated by Nico Clairmont, Irvine, California.

The website

This site has no accounts, no forms, no comments and no tracking scripts. We do not collect, store or sell personal information from visitors. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) under GitHub's privacy statement.

Some pages may contain affiliate links. If you click one, the retailer (for example Amazon) may set its own cookies to attribute a purchase. Those cookies are governed by the retailer's privacy policy.

Our publishing tools (Pinterest and other platform APIs)

Our internal tools connect only to social media accounts that we own. They are used to create and schedule our own posts and pins and to read performance analytics for our own content. They do not access, collect or store data about other users. Access tokens for our own accounts are stored privately on our own computer and are never shared or sold.

Children

This site and our tools are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
