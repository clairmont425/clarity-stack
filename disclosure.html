Affiliate links

I intend to join affiliate programs for software I already pay for and use. When that happens:

  • Every affiliate link will be marked at the link itself, not only in a footer.
  • Every page containing one will carry a disclosure above the content, before you read the recommendation rather than after.
  • Commission never buys a verdict. The tools I'd most like to be paid by are the ones I've criticised hardest on this site. If a program's terms required editorial control, I wouldn't join it.
  • No link cloaking or shorteners. You'll always see where a link goes.
  • If I ever get paid for placement — a sponsored post, a paid review — it would be labelled as advertising in the headline, not buried. I have no such arrangements today.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. — this sentence is required of Amazon Associates and is published here for completeness; I am not currently an Amazon Associate and no Amazon link appears on this site yet.

AI-generated content

  • The video host is an AI-generated animated character. She is labelled as AI-generated on every platform that provides the label, and she is visibly illustrated rather than photoreal, specifically so she can't be mistaken for a real customer.
  • The host never gives a testimonial. She explains and demonstrates. She does not say she used, bought, loved or tried anything. The FTC's Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule (16 CFR 465, in force since 21 October 2024) bans fake and AI-generated reviews and testimonials from anyone with no actual experience of the product — correctly, in my view.
  • Experience claims are mine. When a video or page says a tool was run, tested or abandoned, that's me, a real person, describing real work.
  • Demonstrations are real captures. What you see on screen in a demo is an actual recording of the actual tool. I don't generate footage of software interfaces, and I don't generate images of products in someone's hands.
  • Written words are mine, edited with the help of the same AI tools I review — which, given the subject matter, would be strange to hide.

What I won't publish

  • Reviews of things I haven't run.
  • Fabricated product photography or video of physical goods I don't have.
  • Income claims, "passive income" framing, or earnings screenshots.
  • Health, medical or financial advice. Not my field.
  • Fake urgency, fake scarcity, or fake review counts.

Corrections

Factual errors get fixed on the page with a note about what changed. Prices here are dated on purpose — if you find a stale one, that's a bug worth reporting.
