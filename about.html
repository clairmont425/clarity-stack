About

I'm Nico Clairmont. I run Clarity Productions, a small AI video studio in Irvine, California, after about fifteen years in professional production. Clarity Stack is where I write down what the AI side of that work actually taught me.

Why this site exists

I build content pipelines: generated presenters, synthetic narration, image-to-video, automated assembly and publishing. Doing that means paying for a stack of AI tools and hitting their real limits — not the limits in the launch video. Almost everything written about these tools is either a launch announcement or an affiliate roundup by someone who opened a trial that morning. The notes I wanted didn't exist, so these are them.

What makes this worth your time

  • I'm a customer, not a reviewer. The tools here are in weekly production use and I pay for them.
  • The failures are published. Four lip-sync approaches that didn't ship. A video model I had to abandon for one job. That's the expensive knowledge.
  • Production background. Fifteen years of knowing what "good" sounds and looks like is the reason I can tell you a model's face is unusable rather than impressive.
  • I'll tell you what I don't know. See how I test, which includes a list of my own weak evidence.

About the video host

My videos feature an animated AI host. She is clearly an illustrated character, labelled as AI-generated on every platform that offers the label, and she never claims to have used anything — she narrates and explains while the screen shows the actual tool. Any statement of personal experience on this site or in those videos is mine, as me. That's partly the law and mostly just the only version of this that's worth anything.
